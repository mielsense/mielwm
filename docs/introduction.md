# Introduction

mielwm tiles your windows into numbered workspaces, the way AeroSpace does, on one canvas you can zoom out of to see every window at once.

![Every workspace on the canvas, zoomed out](images/overview-dark.jpg)

## Start here

mielwm runs on macOS 15 or later. It needs two permissions, and the keys follow AeroSpace's, so `⌥1` goes to workspace 1 and `⌥/` tiles. These pages take you from the download to your first workspace switch.

- [Install](install.md)
- [First steps](first-steps.md)

## Use it every day

A workspace holds a few windows and lays them out as tiles, as a stack, or lets them float. You switch with `⌥1` to `⌥9` or a three-finger swipe, and zoom out with `⌃⌥G` when you've lost track of something.
