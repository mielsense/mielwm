# Code of conduct

mielwm is a small project, and everyone who takes part should be glad they did. This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews and anywhere else you speak for or about the project.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most people are here to help.
- Criticize the code or the idea, never the person.
- Accept a "no" on a feature or a review comment gracefully. You can disagree, with reasons.
- Help newcomers find their way instead of pointing out what they missed.

## What we don't accept

- Insults, harassment or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Comments on someone's identity, background or appearance.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly pushing after you've been asked to stop.

## What happens

Report a problem with Report content, in the menu of the comment, issue or pull request. The maintainer keeps the name of whoever reported it private.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block someone from the project, depending on how serious it is. A first, minor problem usually gets a private word. Harassment gets a block.
